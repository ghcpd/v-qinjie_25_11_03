Secure refactor principle highlights:
- 🔐 **Secrets management**: .env.example, `dotenv`, and `mysql2/promise` pool usage replace hardcoded credentials.
- 🛡️ **Request hardening**: Helmet middleware, 100-req/15-min rate limit, and JSON body size cap added.
- 🧼 **Input validation**: Username must be 3–64 chars, alphanumeric with `._@-`, otherwise 400 response.
- 🧪 **Prepared statements**: Queries executed with placeholders prevent injection even with crafted payloads.
- 📝 **Error handling**: Deterministic JSON error responses avoid leaking stack traces.
